# Scene depth and effect interactions

User clarification, 2026-10-18: foreground readability must retain world interaction and real light casting. Some cosmetic layers can sit slightly in front of a platform. This supersedes the earlier blanket foreground-camera policy.

All three current scenes include TransparentFX in their normal Base camera. The extra Clear Depth overlay has been removed. Native depth testing, opaque/depth sampling, collisions and receiver illumination remain available. TransparentFX groups effect renderers without determining their render pass. Collider roots keep their gameplay layers. Common emitters retain existing materials and native Particle System/VFX Graph simulation; no ZTest Always or global depth bypass is used.

VfxSurfaceDepthSettings is optional authored placement data: platform layers, probe radius, .06-unit surface clearance and a 1.25-unit maximum shift. The side-on game's physics plane is XY, viewed from negative Z. VfxSurfaceDepth uses native overlap queries to find a nearby platform's front Z boundary and offsets eligible emitter branches just in front, within that bound. It fills the concrete gap that a native renderer sorting order cannot position a cosmetic emitter relative to a collider surface. No nearby/reachable surface means no extra offset. Remove the component/reference for an effect that needs exact world placement.

Only cosmetic particle branches are eligible. Branches containing native particle collision, trigger or lights modules, Collider, Rigidbody or Light components are excluded. Nested subemitter branches stay with their parent. The effect root, native lights, physical contacts, damage/source positions and collision masks remain unchanged. Existing fire profiles retain their authored visual offsets. World-space emitted tails retain their simulated positions when a following source moves; a following aura can update its cosmetic offset. Do not use this utility to move a physical projectile or interacting particle path.

The ten water/fire prefabs have the optional profile; their authoring helpers preserve it. Contact sparks and colliding flame/coals retain their actual paths. Existing fire point lights and their native animation are preserved. The later Zero water lighting revision adds source-local native point lights and Animator envelopes as sibling branches excluded from cosmetic depth adjustment; water art remains unchanged. See [water authoring](../ZeroWater/Authoring.md). Screen-space UI remains above the normal world camera.

Manual Play Mode observation advanced frames 314-359 and game time 5.89-7.01 seconds. The fountain root stayed at (0,0,0); its first emitter moved to Z -.86, including its authored -.30 placement, against a platform front at -.50. The additional shift was -.56. It rendered across that nearby platform while normal world occlusion remained visible. A frozen native fire preview with its existing light enabled/disabled showed receiver illumination on the actual mecha and nearby native Lit ground. The light retained intensity 9.82/range 4.5 and its authored position; collision-enabled emitter transforms remained unchanged. This is not a new per-particle bounce validation. Evidence: ignored Logs/WorldVfx. Compilation and console checks passed, and the clean scene was restored to stopped Edit Mode.

No new shader, source art, vendor effect, network session, build or automated test was needed. Native light/particle budgets are unchanged; no performance benchmark is claimed. The query runs only for opted-in effects when placed or following a source. Visual acceptance remains with the user.
