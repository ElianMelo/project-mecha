# PVP lobby and matches

Configuration has a separate **PVP Lobby** entry. The existing cooperative lobby and route-planning flow remain. PVP uses the installed Unity Multiplayer Services session API for discovery/readiness and FishNet for gameplay; it adds no networking dependency or second transport.

Host-disconnect behavior has a deferred follow-up: 1v1 PVP should end and return to the menu; Team PVP with more than two participants before departure should preserve the match through a successor host. Cooperative continuity is also required. FishNet currently has no documented built-in migration, including no advertised Pro migration feature. See [accepted requirements and deferred scope](HostMigration.md); these outcomes are not implemented by the current lobby.

## Rules and presentation

- New PVP browsers default to **Free-for-All**. The host can switch to **Team** before deployment. Team players choose **Blue** or **Red**; matches need both colors and at least two players. Additional teams are not implemented.
- All pilots must confirm readiness for the current rules revision. Changing rules invalidates old confirmations; changing a pilot's team clears that pilot's readiness. Cooperative and PVP discovery use separate indexed game types; joining a code from the wrong browser is rejected.
- The arena reuses the native opening-room construction, without enemies, exit portal or route voting. Combat waits for the expected network roster and character selection, then runs for **300 seconds**. A connection timeout cancels deployment rather than starting with an incomplete roster. The timer uses the host's monotonic clock, independent of rendered frames.
- Death disables combat/movement until a **5-second** host-controlled respawn. Free-for-All chooses a separated floor spawn; teams spawn on opposite sides. Existing respawn protection remains. Falls count as deaths without a killer or assist. A match ends at the timer deadline; combat closes and final standings persist until players leave through the menu.
- A table directly below chat displays rank, pilot, **K / D / A**, time, team scores, respawn countdown and the final result. Ranking is kills descending, deaths ascending, assists descending, then stable player ID for display order. Equal competitive metrics produce a draw. Team victory uses total team kills. Disconnected pilots remain in the standings and their existing team score remains.
- An assist requires at least **20% of the victim's maximum health in actual damage during that life**, and a different player must deliver the kill. The killer receives no additional assist. Invulnerability/dash rejections do not contribute. Respawning clears life contributions; floating-point comparison includes equality at the threshold.
- The existing world Canvas/Image health-bar presentation is also authored on players and shown only in PVP. Pilot and bar colors identify Blue/Red. Acquisition, melee, skill envelopes and projectile contacts share the same opponent filter; friendly fire, self hits and targeting allies are excluded. Destructible scenery remains usable; players have higher acquisition priority.
- All new static and dynamic game wording comes from the English Lobby String Table and localized font bindings. Existing uGUI navigation scopes and selection frames handle controllers, including motion-disabled focus and the on-screen keyboard. Gameplay input and attacks are blocked during deployment and after the result.

## Responsibility and trust

`PvpMatchSettings` is the read-only ScriptableObject profile at `Assets/_Project/Settings/Network/PvpMatch.asset`. It owns duration, respawn, assist fraction, snapshot cadence, roster timeout, spawn spacing, team colors and coarse position-validation limits. Runtime membership, scores, contributions and deadlines live in `PvpMatchDirector`; shared profiles are never mutated by gameplay.

Only the host applies damage, awards K/D/A, spends weapon resources, schedules attacks, creates damaging projectiles and respawns players. FishNet owner RPCs authenticate the connection/object relationship. Clients submit action intentions and a one-time valid team choice before combat; they cannot submit damage values, scores, respawn deadlines or a team switch during play. Buffered host snapshots synchronize standings and phase; the host closes damage at the exact deadline. Late match connections are refused. Aim updates are rate-limited and reject non-finite values. Existing host-side ammo, mana, cooldown, attack/cancel and dash checks remain.

Movement still uses the existing owner-controlled NetworkTransform. PVP adds non-finite/depth/bounds checks, generous displacement limits, host correction and a temporary damage gate after an invalid position. These are coarse abuse checks, not authoritative movement simulation or lag compensation. Team registration does not cryptographically bind a gameplay connection to its Unity-services account; a modified client may misrepresent its pre-match team choice. The host remains trusted and can manipulate its own simulation. Dedicated authoritative simulation and stronger account-to-connection authentication remain future work.

`NetworkSessionDiagnostics` includes received PVP phase, time and scores when launched with the existing development logging option. `AgentScripts/PvpSessionInspection.cs` supplies operator-invoked snapshots, a local host action and a hidden local peer launcher. It contains no automated test assertions or test runner. `AgentScripts/BuildPvpLobby.cs` authors native UI, settings, scene bindings, player health bars and localization through Unity APIs.

## Validation record

Compilation, a Windows development build, native UI inspections and targeted local multi-process gameplay inspection are used. No automated tests are created, modified or run. Captures, peer logs and build output are local ignored artifacts.

On 2026-10-18, compilation completed without errors and two targeted Windows development builds succeeded. The second build includes the observed assist-boundary correction and received-state diagnostics. Its five warnings are four existing FishNet source warnings plus the optional Pipeline runtime endpoint being disabled; gameplay does not need that endpoint.

Operator inspection used the Editor host and two instances of one development build. The Free-for-All run completed the full 300-second clock, displayed the final winner and rejected further damage. A normal owner weapon request reduced a remote pilot from 100 to 92 health, and melee reduced the remote pilot in 18-point hits. Five-second respawn deadlines and full-health revival were observed. In Team mode, a Blue-to-Blue damage attempt left health unchanged; a shot through a Blue ally acquired and damaged Red while Blue remained at 100 health. Exactly 20 damage earned an assist on another pilot's kill; 19 damage in the next life earned none. Both peers' received diagnostics matched the host's K/D/A and team state. A post-start team-change request remained Blue, and an out-of-bounds transform was corrected to its last valid position. No additional multi-client or remote Relay session was run for the later label-only refinement.

A temporary online PVP lobby was created and deleted through the normal service flow. Its indexed game type was separate from co-op, its default mode was Free-for-All, a single ready pilot could not start PVP, switching to Team invalidated readiness and changed the rules revision, and selecting Red persisted in the roster. The PVP browser/room, focus routes, native font bindings and selected-team wording were inspected. A final cooperative host opened the existing route-choice flow with PVP inactive and player PVP health bars hidden. MainScene and stopped Play Mode were restored, the temporary lobby was deleted and all validation peer processes were stopped. Full internet-match latency, loss, account binding and dedicated authority remain outside these checks.
