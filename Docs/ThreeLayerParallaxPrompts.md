# Three-layer cyberpunk art prompts

Created 2026-10-14 using the built-in image generation tool, one generation per image. The middle and near images were requested with transparent backgrounds and their original alpha was preserved. All three are 1536 × 1024 PNGs. No models were generated or changed.

The files below are saved in `Assets/_Project/Art/Environment/`. Native Sprite assets of the same names reference these PNGs; `ParallaxScene.asset` references all three. The earlier CyberpunkCity image remains available as historical art but is no longer used by this scene.

## CyberpunkFarSky.png

Mode: built-in generation. Transparent background: false.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: far background layer for a side-scrolling cyberpunk mecha game, landscape 3:2 image.
Primary request: hand-painted 1980s anime cyberpunk night skyline viewed straight on, distant tiny futuristic city towers along the lower third, broad atmospheric deep blue and muted violet sky, cyan haze above roofs, sparse warm amber windows, cel-painted hard shadow shapes with subtle painted texture. Keep the middle and upper areas open and calm for readable gameplay. Horizon at lower third. This is the far opaque layer of a three-layer parallax scene. No foreground buildings, no characters, no ground/platforms, no text, no logos, no UI, no watermark. Full bleed opaque background.
```

## CyberpunkMiddleSkyline.png

Mode: built-in generation. Transparent background: true.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: middle-distance transparent parallax background layer for a side-scrolling cyberpunk mecha game, landscape 3:2.
Primary request: isolated continuous futuristic industrial city skyline, straight-on orthographic side view, hand-painted 1980s mecha anime cel background aesthetic. Clustered dark desaturated blue steel towers, rooftop vents, antennas and connecting skybridges; restrained cyan and warm amber windows. Buildings rise from the bottom edge and occupy the lower half with occasional taller towers to two thirds. Broad open transparent space above and between silhouettes. Hard painted shadow shapes, muted contrast so characters remain readable. Match a deep blue/violet night city. Genuinely transparent alpha sky/background; no opaque sky or backdrop, no ground/platform, no characters, no readable text, logos, watermark or UI. Clean silhouette edges, full-width skyline reaching bottom and both side edges.
```

## CyberpunkNearRooftops.png

Mode: built-in generation. Transparent background: true.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: near background transparent parallax layer for a side-scrolling cyberpunk mecha game, wide landscape 3:2.
Primary request: a low industrial rooftop silhouette strip, straight-on orthographic side view, hand-painted 1980s anime cel background. Dark blue gray rooftop ventilation ducts, short machinery housings, cables, pipe elbows and small antenna structures, occasional restrained cyan status lamps and amber windows. Objects occupy only the bottom quarter of the image, a few narrow antennas to the middle, leaving the upper three quarters and center largely empty transparent space for gameplay. Hard cel shadow shapes, subtle painted steel texture, readable blue gray edges against deep blue night. A continuous strip across the full width at the bottom. Actual transparent alpha background, no sky, no opaque backdrop, no full-height pillars or walls, no characters, no gameplay platforms, no text, no logo, no watermark or UI. This is scenery behind the player, not a playable floor.
```
